# HOMEONLY3 — Diagnostic containment after Home exits

Date: 2026-09-29

## Corrected device sequence

The previous interpretation incorrectly treated the final transition to the iOS
Home Screen as an EKA2L1 crash.

The tester clarified the real sequence:
1. Menu3 launched the real Home Screen.
2. Home disappeared and EKA2L1 returned to its app library.
3. About 1–2 seconds later the tester manually swiped to the iOS Home Screen.
4. The tester then reopened EKA2L1 and opened Menu3 to verify MANIC skin.
5. That second guest session appended new data and contaminated the original
   Home-failure boundary in the logs.

Therefore:
- there is NO evidence that the iOS EKA2L1 process crashed after the Home test;
- the real observed failure remains Home disappearing back to the EKA2L1 app list;
- the final Home guest panic/exit boundary was not captured cleanly because a new
  guest session was started afterwards.

## Why HOMEONLY3 still has diagnostic value

The historical iOS frontend implements:

`onAppExited -> exitGame -> bridge::exit_game -> shutdown/restart emulator`

for every launched guest UID.

For M3HOME:
- Menu3 is deliberately kept alive;
- Home UID 0x102750F0 is launched on top of Menu3;
- once Home has already exited/panicked, the stock callback reboots the emulator
  and returns to the host app library.

HOMEONLY3 changes ONLY this post-exit host reaction for Home UID 0x102750F0.

It does NOT execute before Home exits and therefore is not intended to fix or
bypass the root Home failure.

HOMEONLY3:
- does NOT call `exitGame()` for Home UID after Home exits;
- does NOT call `bridge::exit_game()` for Home UID after Home exits;
- does NOT modify CenRep or guest/kernel behavior;
- restores host bookkeeping to Menu3 UID 0x101F4CD2;
- keeps the existing Menu3 emulator session alive;
- leaves the actual Home panic/exit untouched in the logs.

All non-Home guest exits keep the original behavior.

## Status

HOMEONLY2 remains the authoritative semantic baseline:
- MENUUI36 WINFOCUS1
- NOJAVA
- MANIC3
- M3HOME1 Menu3 -> Home UID 0x102750F0
- CenRep transaction/DeleteRange support
- GetString diagnostic probe
- NativeBoot absent
- CompatBoot absent
- J2ME absent

HOMEONLY3 is a diagnostic containment build derived from HOMEONLY2. It must not
be described as a fix for an iOS crash, because no iOS crash was observed.

## Clean next test

HOMEONLY3 may be used specifically to preserve the failure boundary.

1. Install HOMEONLY3 over HOMEONLY2.
2. Open Menu3 and confirm touch still works.
3. Choose **Vào màn hình chính**.
4. Do not touch anything after Home disappears.
5. Do not reopen Menu3, do not relaunch another guest app, and do not use the
   game menu after the failure.
6. Leave EKA2L1 in that state for roughly 10 seconds so buffered log writes finish.
7. Export/copy the EKA2L1 logs immediately.
8. Only after copying logs may the tester swipe to iOS Home.
9. Video may be stopped immediately after the post-Home state is visible.

If HOMEONLY3 works as intended, the key marker should be:
`[HOMEONLY3][HOME_EXIT_KEEP_MENU3]`

The primary target is still the exact final Home guest panic/exit boundary.
